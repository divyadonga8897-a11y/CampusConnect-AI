Sri Satya Institute Scholarships & Concessions:

1. Merit Excellence Scholarship:
   - Eligibility: Students securing 90% or above in 10+2 / Intermediate exams, or in the top 1000 ranks in EAMCET.
   - Benefit: 50% tuition fee waiver for the first year, renewable based on maintaining a CGPA of 8.5+.
2. SC/ST/BC Community Reimbursement:
   - Eligibility: Government-defined eligibility rules (income limits below 2.5 Lakhs annually).
   - Benefit: Full or partial tuition fee reimbursement credited directly via the State welfare department.
3. Sports Concessions:
   - Eligibility: Representing state or national teams in recognized sports leagues.
   - Benefit: 25% waiver on annual tuition fees.
4. Required Documents: Submit caste certificate, income statement, 10+2 marks memo, and study certificate during admission counseling.
