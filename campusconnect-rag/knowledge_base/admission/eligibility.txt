Sri Satya Institute Admission Eligibility:

1. Academic Qualification: Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (MPC) subjects from a recognized board.
2. Minimum Marks: Candidates must secure a minimum of 60% aggregate marks in MPC subjects.
3. Entrance Exams: Candidates must qualify in EAMCET or have a valid JEE Mains rank card.
4. Required Documents for Verification:
   - 10th / SSC Memo of Marks
   - 10+2 / Intermediate Memo of Marks
   - EAMCET / JEE Rank Card & Hall Ticket
   - Transfer Certificate (TC) & Study Certificates (Class 6 to 12)
   - Caste Certificate (if applicable)
   - Income Certificate (if applying for fee reimbursement)
   - Aadhar Card copy
