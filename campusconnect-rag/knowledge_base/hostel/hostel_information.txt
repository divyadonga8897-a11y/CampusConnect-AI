Sri Satya Institute Hostel Facilities & Rules:

We provide separate residential blocks for Boys and Girls on the campus:
1. Rooms: Double and Triple sharing rooms fully furnished with individual wooden beds, mattresses, study tables, chairs, and cupboards.
2. Dining Mess: Hygienic and modern kitchen facilities preparing nutritious vegetarian and non-vegetarian food mapped by student mess committees.
3. Security: 24/7 active gate security, biometric logging systems, resident wardens, and CCTV cameras.
4. Amenities:
   - High-speed campus Wi-Fi connectivity
   - Late-hour reading cabins and digital library links
   - Indoor recreation space (table tennis, chess, carrom)
   - Continuous RO purified drinking water supply
   - First-aid clinic on-call doctors
